Vehicle Profile Flags
=====================

Vehicle profiles define routing rules for Navit. A road must at least have certain flags to be considered for routing.


Flag Definitions
----------------

The following flags are used in vehicle profiles:

+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Bit  | Name                | Description                                                        |
+======+=====================+====================================================================+
| 0    | ONEWAY              | Road allows travel in one direction only                           |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1    | ONEWAYREV           | Road allows travel in reverse direction only                       |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2    | SEGMENTED           | Road is segmented                                                  |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3    | ROUNDABOUT          | Road is a roundabout                                               |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4    | ROUNDABOUT_VALID    | Roundabout is valid for routing                                    |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5    | ONEWAY_EXCEPTION    | Exception to one-way restriction                                   |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6    | SPEED_LIMIT         | Road has a speed limit                                             |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 7    | RESERVED            | Reserved                                                           |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 8    | SIZE_OR_WEIGHT_LIMIT| Road has size or weight restrictions                               |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 9    | THROUGH_TRAFFIC_LIMIT| Through traffic is limited                                        |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 10   | TOLL                | Road requires toll payment                                         |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 11   | SEASONAL            | Road is only available seasonally                                  |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 12   | UNPAVED             | Road is unpaved                                                    |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 13   | FORD                | Road crosses a ford                                                |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 14   | UNDERGROUND         | Road is underground                                                |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 19   | DANGEROUS_GOODS     | Road allows dangerous goods transport                              |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 20   | EMERGENCY_VEHICLES  | Road allows emergency vehicles                                     |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 21   | TRANSPORT_TRUCK     | Road allows transport trucks                                       |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 22   | DELIVERY_TRUCK      | Road allows delivery trucks                                        |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 23   | PUBLIC_BUS          | Road allows public buses                                           |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 24   | TAXI                | Road allows taxis                                                  |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 25   | HIGH_OCCUPANCY_CAR  | Road allows high occupancy vehicles                                |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 26   | CAR                 | Road allows cars                                                   |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 27   | MOTORCYCLE          | Road allows motorcycles                                            |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 28   | MOPED               | Road allows mopeds                                                 |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 29   | HORSE               | Road allows horses                                                 |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 30   | BIKE                | Road allows bicycles                                               |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 31   | PEDESTRIAN          | Road allows pedestrians                                            |
+------+---------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+


Examples
--------

For cars, use ``0x04000000`` (bit 26 = CAR).

For bicycles, use ``0x40000000`` (bit 30 = BIKE).

For pedestrians, use ``0x80000000`` (bit 31 = PEDESTRIAN).
